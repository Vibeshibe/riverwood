A place to stay awhile.

Riverwood is an independent social federation network for furries and friends. Its Mastodon instance is the public square for posts and wider fediverse conversations. Its Matrix instance is the quieter place for rooms and real-time chat.

Matrix uses the Riverwood Mastodon account for authentication. Members create one account, keep one identity, and can move between both services without another password to remember.

The town’s autumn streets and strange, familiar warmth draw longstanding inspiration from the Longest Night universe. Riverwood is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the creators or publishers of Night in the Woods.
